Process

From first contact to closing

Every step is documented. Asset details are released only after a signed confidentiality agreement.

  1. TeaserAsset type, size, location band and price range. No name, no address.
  2. NDAConfidentiality and fee agreement signed by the investor.
  3. MemorandumTitle, planning, financial model and photographs.
  4. Visit and diligenceSite visit, meetings with owner, lawyers and the town hall.
  5. Offer and closingNegotiation support through to the notary.
