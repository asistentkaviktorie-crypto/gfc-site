Who has bought what

Published deals only. Green Future Capital was not a party to these transactions.

October 2026

Spanish buyers lead hotel investment

Spanish capital accounted for 62% of hotel investment in the first half of 2026, with five-star hotels taking 51% of the total.

Source: Colliers
August 2026

La Zambra in Mijas changes hands

Grupo Inversor Hesperia bought the 197-room La Zambra resort from Intriva Capital, which had acquired the closed Byblos hotel in 2019 and reopened it in 2022.

Source: HVS
July 2026

Record half-year for Costa del Sol hotels

Hotel investment on the Costa del Sol reached €435 million between January and June, second only to the Balearic Islands and 18% of the national total.

Source: Colliers
First half 2026

Land for a Four Seasons in Marbella

Immobel and Fort Partners acquired 90% of the hotel land for the future Four Seasons Marbella.

Source: El Español
May 2026

St. Regis resort approved in Las Chapas

Marbella approved the urbanisation licence for Las Dunas, a 169-room St. Regis hotel with 151 residences on a 160,000 m² beachfront plot, promoted by Platinum Estates and Sierra Blanca Estates. Opening is planned for 2031.

Source: RLI
August 2025

Fairmont La Hacienda sold for €175 million

ActivumSG acquired the newly opened resort in San Roque, with 311 keys including branded residences.

Source: Hospitality Net
December 2024

Modon Holding buys La Zagaleta

The Abu Dhabi developer acquired 100% of La Zagaleta in Benahavís, its first investment in European luxury real estate.

Source: Zawya
