Add only assets whose owner has agreed in writing to open presentation. Everything else stays off the site and is presented on request.

How to publish

  1. Fill in the form and press “Add asset to the list”. Tick “Show on the site” when the asset may be displayed.
  2. Press “Download listings.js”.
  3. Upload the file to the folder data on the server, replacing the old one. Upload the photo to img/listings.

Nothing changes on the public site until the file is uploaded.
