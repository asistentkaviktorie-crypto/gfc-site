What we source

Assets that institutional buyers can underwrite

Buyers on this coast have paid for position and potential: a new five-star resort in San Roque, a rebuilt 1980s hotel in Mijas, a beachfront plot in Marbella for a branded hotel with residences. We look for the next assets of that kind before they reach an open process.

  • 4 and 5 star hotels from 100 keys
  • Closed or dated hotels with a repositioning case
  • Resort hotels with room for branded residences
  • Beachfront and golf locations
  • Sale, sale and manage-back, or joint venture

What you receive

A file ready for an investment committee

  • Operating figures for three years where the hotel trades
  • Licence, classification and planning position
  • Capital expenditure estimate from a local technical adviser
  • Operator and brand options
  • Seller's price expectation and timetable

Who buys here

International and Spanish capital

Recent buyers and lenders on this coast include private equity funds, hotel owner-operators, Gulf developers and Spanish family offices. In the first half of 2026 Spanish buyers accounted for 62% of hotel investment in the country, according to Colliers.
