What we source

Land a developer can start on

Gulf and international developers entering this coast buy land and build themselves. They need plots where the planning question is already answered. We check each plot against the municipal plan before it is presented.

  • Urban land or land with an approved partial plan
  • Hotel, tourist-residential or residential use
  • Sea view, front line or golf frontage
  • From 10,000 m² of buildable area
  • Clear title, utilities and access

What we verify first

Planning before price

  • Classification in the general plan (PGOU)
  • Buildability, use and height limits
  • Status of urbanisation works and charges
  • Coastal and environmental restrictions
  • Land registry and cadastre match

Structures

Purchase, option or joint venture

Owners on this coast often prefer a partner to an outright sale. We can present a direct purchase, a purchase option that gives time for diligence, or a joint venture in which the landowner contributes the plot.
