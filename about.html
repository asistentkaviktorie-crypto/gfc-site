About

Founder

Viktoria Kostebelova

Founder, Green Future Capital

Viktoria has worked in real estate for 25 years, first in Russia and then for fifteen years in the Czech Republic and Germany. She is now based in Sotogrande and works the coast between Málaga and Gibraltar, where she meets owners and developers in person.

How we work

Principles

  • Written mandate before any asset is presented
  • Confidentiality agreement before any detail is shared
  • Client identification under Spanish anti-money-laundering rules
  • Independent legal advice for every party
  • Fees agreed in writing and paid on closing

What we are not

Clear limits

Green Future Capital is not a fund manager and does not take deposits, manage money or offer collective investment products. We introduce assets and counterparties and support the transaction.
